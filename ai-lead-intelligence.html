01 / The problem

Manual triage made every lead a judgment call.

What was broken

Raw lead batches contained duplicates, missing fields, inconsistent wording, and unstructured conversations. Reps had to manually clean records, assess relevance, infer intent, identify objections, rank prospects, and draft outreach.

Product goal

Automate the journey from raw leads to actionable sales intelligence without hiding uncertainty. Clean and enrich records, prioritize consistently, and send questionable outputs to a human review queue.

02 / Solution design

A six-stage pipeline with clear responsibilities.

Two deterministic rule stages handle data cleaning and priority scoring. Four OpenAI-driven stages classify, enrich, draft outreach, and independently evaluate quality.

01 · RULES

Clean & normalize

Validate email and phone formats, normalize German proficiency, flag missing or invalid fields, and detect duplicates using normalized email or phone—not names.

02 · AI

Classify relevance

Return Yes, No, or Review with confidence, reason, and evidence tied to the individual lead.

03 · AI

Enrich intent

Summarize profile, intent, potential needs, objections, missing information, opportunities, and next action.

04 · RULES

Prioritize leads

Compute a 0–100 score from relevance, intent, German level, urgency, buying signal, and data confidence.

05 · AI

Draft outreach

Create lead-specific outreach; ask a clarifying question for Review, close respectfully for No, and respect opt-outs.

06 · AI + CODE

Evaluate quality

Score grounding, intent, completeness, consistency, outreach fit, and uncertainty handling; code applies the final decision.

03 / Trust & quality

Evidence first. Uncertainty visible.

Evidence provenance

Evidence must match the same lead's own data, using exact or bounded-fuzzy matching. Cross-lead evidence borrowing and unsupported claims are rejected.

Independent evaluator

A separate model call scores six quality dimensions. Code computes the weighted score: PASS at 90 or above, REVIEW at 75 or above, and FAIL below 75. Hard flags force FAIL.

Never silently drop a lead

If an AI output fails validation, an honest placeholder replaces the affected field. The lead continues through later stages and is flagged for review.

Separate review queues

Uncertain or failed records go to human review; confirmed duplicates go to their own queue so repeats do not bury judgment calls.

Reported run: 120 evidence and signal checks across 30 leads and 12 OpenAI calls. Three checks required fallback—one malformed output and two unverified quotes—and zero leads were lost.

05 / Metrics & guardrails

Measure value without averaging away risk.

North-star metric

Ready-to-Send Rate: the share of leads classified Yes and given a PASS quality decision, requiring no manual review.

Supporting metrics

Average evaluator score, qualification rate, and priority precision help reveal quality changes and whether triage is meaningfully discriminating between leads.

Non-negotiable guardrails

Zero-drop rate, zero duplicate-leak rate, zero evidence-fabrication rate, and hard-flag integrity.

Failure modes considered

Hallucinated claims, transient API errors, prompt injection in free text, duplicate outreach, and reviewers overwhelmed by mixing duplicates with ambiguous leads.

06 / Implementation

Rules decide what can be trusted.

The documented prototype uses a React + Vite frontend, a Node/Express orchestrator, and the OpenAI API, with system prompts externalized in configuration. Deterministic code validates evidence and computes scoring decisions; AI generates the content and summaries.
